Mouse & keyboard help
  • Click or drag to paint with the current tool.
  • Right-click / right-drag removes what the current tool paints.
  • Roads and rivers in cells follow the path you drag, cell to cell, and the ends join any road or river next to them. Clicking a single cell connects it to its neighbors. Drag past the map edge to have them run off the map.
  • Cliffs, and rivers on borders: clicking one that already exists removes it.
  • Cliff on border: the teeth point to the low side. Draw with the pointer on the low side, or Shift+click a cliff to flip it.
  • Sites sit on top of terrain; the cell keeps its terrain and movement. The terrain icon shrinks into the corner.
  • Labels: with the Label tool, click to add, drag to move, click to edit, right-click to delete. "Attach to cell" labels sit under their cell and snap from cell to cell.
  • 1–9, 0, U pick a terrain. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo.
  • Ctrl+scroll or + / − to zoom. Space+drag or middle-drag to pan.
  • Your map saves itself in this browser. Use Save file to keep a copy or move it to another computer.
